STEP 1

This isn't regular checked baggage

GoodLugg ships your bags as air cargo — not at the check-in counter, but through a separate freight channel.

🧳
Checked baggage
You hand it over at the airport
More lenient rules
📦
Air cargo (GoodLugg)
Shipped separately as freight
Stricter regulations
Why stricter? Air cargo travels without a passenger. If something goes wrong, there's no one on board to respond — so the rules are tighter.
Here's what can't go in
High-alcohol cosmetics (toner, perfume, sprays)
Anything with a lithium battery (power banks, earbuds, etc.)
Electronics, fire hazards, and more
STEP 2

No toners, perfumes, or sprays

They're high in alcohol, which makes them hazardous for air cargo.
