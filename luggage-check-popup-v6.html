Your bags fly without you

Goodlugg ships your bags as air cargo, in the plane's hold — without you on board. Stricter safety rules apply when bags travel alone.

About the 100ml rule — that's carry-on only. Air cargo has no volume limit, but toner is banned at any size. Pack small liquids in your carry-on to be safe.
The rule Anything with a non-removable lithium battery is banned. Devices with removable batteries are OK — but take the battery out first.
