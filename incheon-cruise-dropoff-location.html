Goodlugg Zone Location

Terminal parking lot, right outside the Arrival Hall.

After immigration and customs, walk out of the Arrival Hall and continue straight into the parking lot. The Goodlugg Zone is under the white tents.

(open: from disembarkation until 13:00)

What to Look For

Look for the white tents in the parking lot, facing the ship. Our staff are there throughout disembarkation.
