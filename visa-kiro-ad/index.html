It stays word for word across all sixteen. What changes is the subtitle, the colour ground, and how much room the tiger gets — so the offer reads the same from any screen while the frames stay worth looking at over a six-month flight.

Eight readings of the same line

Main copy

Every one of these is a rendering of 비자카드만 있으면 굿럭 짐배송 1개 공짜. They differ in what they lead with: the benefit, the low barrier, or Goodlugg as the one doing the delivering. All eight are set with the line breaks locked, so a phrase never splits across two lines.

A

One bag free, with your Visa card.

Benefit first, condition second. Shortest of the eight, so it sets largest.

B

Just your Visa card. One bag free.

“Just” is the closest English to 만 있으면 — the whole point is that this is all it takes.

C

All you need is a Visa card.

The lowest-barrier reading. The offer itself moves to the subtitle.

D

Goodlugg delivers one bag free, with your Visa card.

Goodlugg is the grammatical subject, which is the posture the agreement asks for.

E

Have a Visa card? One bag travels free.

A question stops the eye. “travels” says it is a delivery, not a giveaway suitcase.

F

Any Visa card. One bag free.

Five words. Reads from the far side of a gate lounge.

G

One bag on us, with your Visa card.

Warmest of the eight. “on us” is idiomatic, so it may not land for every reader.

H

One bag delivered free with your Visa card.

The current line. “delivered” removes any doubt about what is being given.

Five ways to say it runs both ways

Seoul ⇄ Busan subtitle

The Seoul–Busan hotel route works in both directions, and the earlier drafts only said one. These five all fix that; they differ in how plainly they spell it out.

1

Seoul and Busan, hotel to hotel, either way.

Plainest English. “either way” is the phrase a non-native reader is most likely to know.

2

Busan to Seoul, or Seoul to Busan. Free.

Spells both directions out. Longest, but nothing is left to inference.

3

Between Seoul and Busan hotels. Both directions.

“Between” already implies both, and the second sentence confirms it.

4

Seoul ⇄ Busan, hotel to hotel.

The arrow does the work in any language, and it is the symbol the route list already uses.

5

Either direction between Seoul and Busan hotels.

Leads on the reassurance rather than the cities.

The point worth pressing

Seoul to Busan is in it too

Goodlugg’s highest-priced Korea route

Seoul hotel → Busan hotel
₩55,000 Free

Hotel to hotel across the country is the most expensive thing on the Korea Luggage Delivery price list — up to ₩55,000 for a single item — and it is included on the same terms as a bag going across Seoul. Four of the sixteen versions (C1 to C4) are built around it and two more carry it in the subtitle. The frames say “up to” because the route is priced by bag size, from ₩49,000 to ₩55,000.
