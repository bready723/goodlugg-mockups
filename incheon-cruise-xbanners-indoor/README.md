# Incheon Cruise indoor X-banners

Live: `https://bready723.github.io/goodlugg-mockups/incheon-cruise-xbanners-indoor/`

Banner for the hall right after disembarkation: book inside, leave the bags at the Goodlugg Zone outside. 6 copies (4A to 4F) on the two fixed backgrounds (Y = yellow, A = purple arch) = 12 banners. Every banner opens with the service name and carries the booking QR with "Book in 5 min.".

Design is matched to the CEO-approved outdoor finals (`../IncheonCruiseBanners.jpeg`): colours sampled from it (purple #5f2079, yellow #f6ed14); `pattern.jpg`, `lockup_yellow.png`, `wordmark_purple.png` and `qr.png` are crops of that image, so they are review quality, not print artwork.

Full size (600 x 1800): `?only=4A-Y`. Fit report: `?qa=1` (over = 0 and wide = 0 on all 12; the copy shrinks to fit, never the QR).

Before print: confirm the QR destination; rebuild the chosen banner in the designer's source file.
